## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
Football Live TV is the fastest way to follow every match — live scores, fixtures, league tables, team news and a TV guide that tells you which channel or streaming service is showing the game in your country.

LIVE SCORES IN REAL TIME
• Minute-by-minute scores from the Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, the Champions League and more
• Goals, red cards, half-time and full-time updates as they happen
• Filter by league, or jump straight to the matches you care about

FIXTURES AND RESULTS
• Every fixture converted to your local timezone
• Yesterday, today and the week ahead in one clean list
• Full results archive with scorers and match stats

LEAGUE TABLES
• Standings that update the moment a match ends
• Points, goal difference, and a recent-form guide for each club
• Title race, European places and relegation battle at a glance

MATCH CENTRE
• Line-ups, formations, possession, shots and cards
• Stadium, kick-off time and referee
• Pre-match odds and win probability, shown for information only

WHERE TO WATCH
• The official broadcasters and streaming services carrying each match in your region
• Never miss a kick-off because you were on the wrong channel

ALERTS THAT DON'T SPAM YOU
• Follow your clubs and get goal alerts, full-time results and kick-off reminders
• Choose the reminder lead time — 5 minutes to 2 hours before kick-off
• Different settings per club and per league, or mute everything with one switch
• Tap an alert and it opens straight to that match

BUILT TO BE FAST
• Dark, low-glare design made for checking scores one-handed
• Light on battery and data

IMPORTANT
Football Live TV is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any club, league, federation or broadcaster. All club names and badges belong to their respective owners.

The app does not host, stream or rebroadcast any match footage. The TV guide points you to the official rights holders in your country.

Odds and win probabilities are displayed for information only. No betting takes place in this app, there is no wagering, no virtual currency and no bookmaker account. 18+.

The app is free and supported by ads.

Questions or feedback: support@footballlivetv.app
```
